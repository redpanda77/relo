Relo for Mac turns the notch into a live view of your agents, scripts and apps. While nothing runs it
stays a plain black notch. When work moves, the black grows a wing on each side of the camera. Hover
over the notch or click it to open the full list.

It shows the same story as your iPhone: Relo decides each card's state (on this Mac, or on your server), and the notch only
draws it. See [Cards](/docs/concepts/cards) for when a card is fresh, folded or gone.

## The closed notch

- **Left wing: who is live.** Relo's mark with one satellite circling it for each live session. A
  session that moved recently circles faster; one that went quiet slows down.
- **Right wing: how far along.** The leading card's plan, one segment per top-level step. The current
  step fills as far as its own steps go, with light running through what is done. `+2` counts the
  other live sessions.
- **The color: the state.** Both wings carry a soft wash in the state's color. The camera housing in
  the middle always stays black.

## States

### Running

Violet. The mark breathes, the satellites circle, and the plan fills toward a glowing head. Nothing
lights the edge: running work never asks for your attention.

### A step finished

The finished step flashes and settles, the mark pulses with it, and two violet beams leave the bottom
of the notch and climb both sides. Every finished step lights up; its sound plays at most every three
minutes per card.

### Needs you

A question waits for you. The mark itself turns coral and pulses, sending rings across the wing,
while the other sessions keep circling. The edge glows, coral beams repeat, and the right wing says
**Answer**. Open the notch to answer right there.

## Beams and the card

Beams mark a moment: a finished step, a question, your turn, a finish or a failure. Two lights leave
the bottom centre together and climb both sides in the state's color, on top of the state's own
motion. Once the moment has played, the notch opens into the update's card for a few seconds: a
question or a failure shows the whole card with its answer buttons, a start or a finish one line.
Turn this off in **Settings › Relo › Expand the notch when work starts, needs you, finishes or fails**.

## Settings

In Relo for Mac's **Settings**:

- **Show Relo in the closed notch** turns the wings on or off.
- **Play sounds** and one switch per kind of update; **Stay quiet while a full-screen app is in front**
  keeps a full-screen app undisturbed.
- **Full screen behavior** (General) hides the closed notch in full screen: for every app, only for the
  app playing media (the default), or never.
- **Show on all displays** draws a notch on every screen; otherwise it follows the main display when
  **Automatically switch displays** is on.

## When the notch shows nothing

The wings show only while something is live. If work runs and the notch stays plain:

- **Nothing is live for the notch.** A card that went quiet or finished a while ago leaves the wings,
  as it leaves your Lock Screen.
- **Full screen.** The app in front is in full screen and **Full screen behavior** hides the notch.
- **Another display.** With an external display, check that the notch you are looking at is the one Relo
  draws on (**Show on all displays**).

Relo for Mac logs every change, with the reason and the screen. Run this on the Mac to see the last hour:

```sh title="On the Mac"
/usr/bin/log show --last 1h --predicate 'subsystem == "relo.notch" AND category == "wings"'
```

Each line reads `wings: shown` or `wings: hidden:` with why, such as `full screen` or `nothing live`.
