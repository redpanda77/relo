Relo brings every agent, script and app you run, wherever it runs, into one place on your iPhone. A
handful of ideas make that work: each source declares its work, the server decides what is worth
your attention, and only you can read the words. These pages explain what each idea is and why it
works the way it does. The guides and the reference say exactly how.

<ul class="docs-tiles">
<li><a href="/docs/concepts/work"><strong>Work, plans and steps</strong>One goal per session or job, a plan that grows as it learns, one honest outcome.</a></li>

<li><a href="/docs/concepts/questions"><strong>Questions and answers</strong>Approve, answer yes or no, or pick an option. No answer means no.</a></li>

<li><a href="/docs/concepts/sessions"><strong>Sessions</strong>One agent conversation, its own work, and your view across all of them.</a></li>

<li><a href="/docs/concepts/encryption"><strong>End-to-end encryption</strong>Sealed on your computer, opened only on your phones. Your server cannot read it.</a></li>

## How the ideas fit

- A producer (an agent session, a script or job, an app or service) declares a
  [work](/docs/concepts/work): a goal, maybe a plan, a status, and in the end an outcome.
- The server derives one [card](/docs/concepts/cards) from it, and decides where it shows and
  whether it alerts. That policy lives in one place: [the three sources of state](/docs/concepts/state).
- When the work depends on you, it [asks](/docs/concepts/questions) on its card. When it ends, its
  outcome is the [notification](/docs/concepts/notifications).
- For agents, the unit is the [session](/docs/concepts/sessions): each sees only its own work, and
  you see them all.
- The words travel [end-to-end encrypted](/docs/concepts/encryption) from the computer to your phones.
