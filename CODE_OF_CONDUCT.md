# Code of conduct

Relo follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work, and make the project
welcoming to people of every background and level of experience. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests, reviews or
any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer on GitHub
([@redpanda77](https://github.com/redpanda77)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove comments, close threads or block participants who break this code.
